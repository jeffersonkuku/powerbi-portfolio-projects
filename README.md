## How I build a dashboard

\`\`\`mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Raw business data] --> B[Clean & organise]
    B --> C[Define useful business rules]
    C --> D[Build clear KPIs]
    D --> E[Show the problem visually]
    E --> F[Help the user decide what to do]
\`\`\`

The dashboard is not the goal by itself. The goal is to make a business situation **faster to understand and easier to act on**.

## What these projects demonstrate

| Business need | What the dashboard helps answer |
|---|---|
| **Budget control** | How much is spent, committed and still available? |
| **Procurement** | Which suppliers, orders or categories need attention? |
| **Service performance** | Are intervention / restoration commitments being met? |
| **Risk** | Where are late orders, backlog, overdue amounts or penalty exposure? |
| **Management reporting** | What should a manager understand first without opening raw files? |

## 🇫🇷 Résumé

Je transforme des données achats, financières ou opérationnelles en tableaux de bord qui permettent de comprendre rapidement :

**ce qui se passe → où est le problème → quelle action regarder en priorité.**

Les détails Power Query, DAX et modèle restent disponibles dans chaque projet, mais ils ne sont plus la première chose à lire.
